About Nusara

Nusara Trading is a Sweden-based trading company specialising in Indonesian agricultural products. We work directly with suppliers across Indonesia and act as a reliable link between origin and buyers abroad.

Our team combines long experience in export and import with a hands-on relationship with each supplier. We start with spices and botanicals and plan to add more Indonesian goods over time.

How we work
